Privacy Policy

Veonotes · Last updated: July 27, 2026

Veonotes ("we", "our", "the app") is a notification and note-taking tool that tells you when YouTube or TikTok channels you follow go live or upload a new video, and lets you take notes on those videos. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password, or, if you choose to sign in with Google, your name, email address, and profile picture as provided by Google.
  • Content you add: the YouTube and TikTok channel URLs you choose to track, and any notes you write on videos (including formatting and timestamps).
  • Notification preferences: your choices for email alerts, sound alerts, and browser push notifications.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any data beyond what's listed above. We do not sell your data to anyone.

How we use this information

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To check the YouTube/TikTok channels you've added and notify you (by email and/or browser notification) when they go live or upload.
  • To store and display the notes you write, tied to the video and timestamp you wrote them at.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your basic profile info (name, email, picture) with us for that purpose only.
  • Resend — delivers the account and notification emails we send you.
  • YouTube and TikTok — we read publicly available channel and video information (like whether a channel is currently live, or its most recent upload) to power notifications. We do not access your YouTube or TikTok account, and this does not require you to connect or authenticate with either platform.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account, and all associated data, at any time by contacting us at the email below — we'll delete it within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Veonotes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
